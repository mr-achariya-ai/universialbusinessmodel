The Engine

LLM-based Decision Intelligence Engine

Three core capabilities work in concert to route every process to the right decision mode with the right level of human involvement.

Reasoning

The engine applies multi-step contextual reasoning across business rules, historical outcomes, and real-time signals to understand the full context of each decision request before acting.

Decision Optimise

Using reinforcement signals and outcome data, the engine continuously refines decision policies — balancing speed, accuracy, risk tolerance, and business value to optimise for the best result.

Decision Route

Based on process maturity, confidence scores, and risk parameters, the router assigns each process to the most appropriate human-AI collaboration mode — from full automation to expert-in-the-loop oversight.

4 Collaboration Modes

Matching human involvement to process maturity

The engine routes each process to the right mode based on accuracy, confidence, and the cost of a wrong decision. Click any card to expand details.

Human Out of the Loop

Decision Automation

Triggered by: High accuracy process
Automation ▼

The AI acts fully autonomously. Decisions are made and executed without human review. This mode is reserved for processes with a long, validated track record of high-accuracy outcomes where the cost of delay exceeds the risk of error.

Example use case Automated invoice matching and payment approval for established vendor relationships with a 99%+ historical accuracy rate — no human sign-off required.
Human Over the Loop

Decision Augment

Triggered by: Confident process
Augment ▼

The AI provides predictions, suggestions, or analysis that a human supervisor reviews at a high level. The human retains final authority but relies on AI-generated insights to act faster and with greater confidence.

Example use case AI surfaces deal risk scores and recommended contract terms for a sales director to approve or override before a proposal is sent — reducing review time by 70%.
Human on the Loop

Decision Support

Triggered by: Learned process
Support ▼

The AI organises data, visualises options, or suggests possible alternatives to help a human make a well-informed decision. The human is actively in control; the AI acts as a highly capable analyst and advisor.

Example use case AI aggregates market data, customer feedback, and competitor pricing into a structured comparison dashboard that a product manager uses to set the final pricing strategy.
Human in the Loop

Decision Observer

Triggered by: New process
Observer ▼

The human is fully in control and makes every decision. The AI observes interactions, captures patterns, and begins building the knowledge base needed to graduate this process toward higher automation over time.

Example use case A newly launched partner onboarding workflow is handled entirely by the partnerships team while the AI logs decision patterns — preparing for future Decision Support mode.
